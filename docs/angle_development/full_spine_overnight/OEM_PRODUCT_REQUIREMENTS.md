## 3. SEAM CONSTRUCTION & LEAK-PROOF BONDING TECHNIQUES

1. **Gusset Perimeter Bonding (Critical Anti-Leak Requirement):**  
   Standard lockstitch sewing creates needle punctures that act as capillary channels for menstrual fluid.  
   *Technical Feasibility Constraint:* Cellulose natural fibers (organic cotton, bamboo viscose) do not melt ultrasonically. Therefore, seam waterproofing must be achieved through:
   - Heat-activated polyurethane seam-sealing tape (0.08 mm thickness, e.g., Bemis / Framis) applied over the perimeter junction; OR
   - Ultrasonic perimeter bonding of the synthetic internal barrier package (TPU membrane + polyester ADL) prior to garment shell assembly.
2. **Body Construction Seaming:**  
   Outer shell panels must be assembled using **4-needle 6-thread flatlock stitching** (`ISO 4915 Stitch Type 607`). Stitch density must be calibrated to **12–14 stitches per inch (SPI)** to ensure dynamic elasticity without thread popping.
3. **Waistband Construction & Stepped-Down Tapering:**  
   3.5 cm width soft-brushed micro-elastic band.  
   *Stepped-Down Gusset Tapering:* To prevent a thick, uncomfortable ridge across the sacrum, the heavy 280 GSM absorbent core (Layer 3) terminates 1.5 cm below the waistband edge. Only the wicking cotton topsheet (Layer 1) and the 0.015 mm waterproof TPU membrane (Layer 4) continue directly into the folded elastic seam.
4. **Leg Cuffs:**  
   Fold-over self-fabric binding with encased 0.5 mm mobilon elastic tape. Must prevent leg edge rolling without forming a tourniquet band on the quadriceps.

### 4.2 Measurable Stage-Gate Acceptance Criteria
A prototype iteration is approved to proceed to clinical/user wear trials only if it meets all of the following laboratory bench gates:
1. **Intake Rate:** 15 mL synthetic menstrual fluid (SMF) or saline absorbed within $< 3.0\text{ seconds}$ without surface pooling.
2. **Compression Rewet:** Under $0.5\text{ psi}$ static load applied for 2 minutes after 40 mL fluid delivery, fluid transfer to standard filter paper must be $\le 0.50\text{ grams}$.
3. **Incline Run-Off:** On a 30-degree incline, zero fluid run-off over the rear waistband edge when 25 mL fluid is dosed at $5\text{ mL/sec}$.
4. **Hydrostatic Resistance:** Unseamed TPU and taped seams must resist $\ge 10,000\text{ mm H}_2\text{O}$ pressure (AATCC 127).
5. **Wash Stability:** Dimensional shrinkage $< 3.0\%$ in length and width after 5 home laundering cycles (cold wash, line dry).

## 5. STANDARDIZED LABORATORY TESTING PROTOCOL

All claimed specifications must be validated according to internationally recognized textile and consumer hygiene standards. Screening tests must be distinguished from confirmatory speciation, and dimensional stability must be tested independently from post-wash functional retention.

*Critical Standard Scopes:*
- **ISO 9073-6:2025 Scope Distinction:** ISO 9073-6 measures raw nonwoven fabric liquid absorption. It evaluates material swatch fluid uptake only and does NOT measure finished garment anatomical fit, dynamic sleeping movement, compression rewet under body load, or finished waistband/cuff leakage.
- **ISO 20999:2026 Screening Limitation:** Measures Total Fluorine (TF) via combustion ion chromatography. TF screening is NOT proof of zero PFAS. If detected above laboratory LOQ, the testing laboratory must recommend and execute complementary speciation (targeted LC-MS/MS or TOF) to differentiate inorganic fluorides from regulated PFAS chemistry.

## 6. WASH CARE & MAINTENANCE LABELING (TEXTILE FTC REQUIREMENTS)

To preserve the wicking matrix and the integrity of the monolithic TPU membrane over 50+ wash cycles, consumer care labels must strictly mandate:
- **Pre-Wash Protocol:** Rinse in cold tap water immediately after overnight use until runoff is clear.
- **Machine Wash:** Cold gentle cycle (maximum 30°C / 86°F) with standard mild detergent.
- **Prohibited Additives:** **STRICTLY NO FABRIC SOFTENER** (quaternary ammonium compounds coat organic cotton wicking fibers and destroy absorbency), **NO CHLORINE BLEACH**, **NO DRY CLEANING**.
- **Drying:** Line hang dry in ambient shade. Do not tumble dry hot; do not iron directly over the absorbent gusset.
