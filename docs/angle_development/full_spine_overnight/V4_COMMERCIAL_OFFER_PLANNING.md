## 3. PROPOSED FIRST-PAIR SLEEP TRIAL POLICY (COMMERCIAL CONCEPT)

To maximize conversion rate on direct-response paid social without incurring catastrophic inventory loss:

- **Commercial Concept Title:** *Proposed 30-Day "First-Pair Sleep Trial"*
- **Operating Mechanism:**
  1. For multi-pack orders (3-Pack or 5-Pack), customers are encouraged to open, wash, wear, and sleep in **Pair #1**.
  2. Pairs #2 through #5 remain sealed in their tamper-evident frosted hygienic polybags.
  3. If the customer is unsatisfied with the fit, comfort, or leak protection of Pair #1 within 30 days of delivery, they may request a full refund of their purchase price.
  4. The customer keeps Pair #1 for hygiene reasons; unopened pairs are returned to the US 3PL facility via prepaid return label.
- **Financial Risk Modeling (Assumption):**
  * Return rate modeled at: **4.0% to 6.0%** of total shipments.
  * Allowance per order: ~$2.10 USD reserved against gross margin dollars.
- **Governance Notice:** This policy remains a **PROPOSED marketing strategy** subject to explicit commercial sign-off by the Operator.
