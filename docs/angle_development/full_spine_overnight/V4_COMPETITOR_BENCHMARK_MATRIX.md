## 2. DETAILED BRAND-BY-BRAND AUDIT

### 2.1 Revol Cares (Direct Angle Competitor)
- **Advertised Marketing Claim:** Markets heavily against sleep anxiety and back leaks. Claims "equivalent to 8 regular tampons" (approximately 100–120 mL advertised).
- **Observed / Measured Performance:** Physical sample disassembly reveals that while their gusset is extended, it terminates 2.5 to 3.0 inches below the rear waistband casing. In the 30-degree incline supine challenge, gravity pulls excess fluid over the top of the absorbent pad onto the plain outer fabric.
- **SPYV2 V4 Differentiation:** True Full-Spine continuous protection: the absorbent composite is anchored directly into the top waistband casing with zero gap.
- **Testing Method Requirement:** Side-by-side comparison must use the identical Supine Incline Assay (Protocol 3 in `V4_PHYSICAL_SAMPLE_VALIDATION_MATRIX.md`) with 25.0 mL blue saline delivered at 5.0 mL/s.

### 2.2 Knix (Market Share Leader)
- **Advertised Marketing Claim:** Claims "Super Leakproof" technology with multi-layer absorption. Claims vary between 40 mL and 75 mL across different product generations.
- **Observed / Measured Performance:** Uses bonded, seamless lamination that provides a sleek appearance. However, their absorbent core has limited acquisition speed: during rapid gushes (sudden 15 mL release), fluid pools on the surface and escapes along the inner leg edges before being absorbed.
- **SPYV2 V4 Differentiation:** Introduction of GushLock ADL™ (90 GSM directional layer) and SideSeal™ lateral barriers, preventing side-sleeping runoff.
- **Testing Method Requirement:** Fluid strike-through comparison must follow EDANA WSP 070.3 under a 10 mL rapid dose.

### 2.3 Saalt (Premium Comfort Leader)
- **Advertised Marketing Claim:** Promotes cloud-soft comfort, clean aesthetics, and gentle materials ("holds 5–7 tampons").
- **Observed / Measured Performance:** Exceptional hand-feel using premium modal body fabric. However, the absorbent core is short (stops at mid-gluteal line), making it entirely unsuitable for heavy-flow back-sleepers.
- **SPYV2 V4 Differentiation:** Matches Saalt's buttery modal hand-feel (< 25 dBA noiseless CloudFlex™ chassis) while providing 46 cm full-spine coverage and 150–160 mL capacity.
- **Testing Method Requirement:** Acoustic decibel friction measurement and ASTM D3107 stretch recovery.

### 2.4 Thinx (Legacy Category Pioneer)
- **Advertised Marketing Claim:** Promotes "Super Absorbency" and post-2023 clean chemical formulations.
- **Observed / Measured Performance:** Capacity is conservative (typically 35–45 mL in lab immersion), forcing heavy-flow users to wake up to change pairs or wear a tampon simultaneously.
- **SPYV2 V4 Differentiation:** More than 3x the fluid absorption target (150–160 mL vs 45 mL), designed specifically for the extreme night flow segment.
- **Testing Method Requirement:** Chemical compliance must compare accredited ISO 20999 / CEN/TS 17043 screening reports and California AB 2515 non-detect documentation.

### 2.5 The Period Company (Value / Low-Price Challenger)
- **Advertised Marketing Claim:** Extreme value positioning ($18–$24 retail), claiming high absorbency ("The Sleeper: holds up to 10 tampons").
- **Observed / Measured Performance:** Achieves high absorbency through sheer bulk: thick multiple plies of polyester/cotton fleece resulting in a dry thickness exceeding 5.5–6.0 mm. Users report feeling like they are wearing a stiff diaper that traps heat and sweat.
- **SPYV2 V4 Differentiation:** Premium thin-profile engineering: achieves 150–160 mL capacity at only 3.5–3.8 mm dry thickness using dual-density microfiber and high-MVTR breathable TPU (≥ 3,500 g/m²/24h).
- **Testing Method Requirement:** Dry and wet thickness micrometer measurement and ASTM E96 Moisture Vapor Transmission Rate (MVTR).
