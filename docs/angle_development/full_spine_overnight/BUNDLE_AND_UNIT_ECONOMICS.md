### 1.1 Rationalizing the 3-Pack Hero Bundle
The 3-pack priced at **$69.95 USD** is the mathematical core of the commercial strategy:
1. **Biological Alignment:** The average American woman experiences 2 to 3 heavy flow nights per menstrual cycle. A single pair forces immediate daily laundering; a 3-pack allows her to complete her heavy days seamlessly with a single laundry cycle.
2. **Margin Protection:** A single pair priced at $29.95 cannot absorb Meta Ads acquisition costs ($25–$35 CAC) without losing money on the front end. The $69.95 price point expands gross profit dollars sufficiently ($45.22 gross margin) to yield positive net contribution on the first purchase even at a $32–$36 CPA.
3. **Competitive Pricing Anchor:** Market leader Knix charges $72.00–$80.00 for a 3-pack; Saalt charges $89.00. Our $69.95 price offers an immediate, compelling price advantage while staying comfortably above low-end Amazon multi-packs ($38–$45) that lack full waistband rear protection.

## 5. FIRST-PURCHASE RISK REVERSAL & CASH CONVERSION CYCLE

1. **Proposed 30-Night "First-Pair Wash-and-Wear" Trial (Subject to Explicit Operator Approval):**  
   To address consumer hesitation ("What if it doesn't fit?"), the proposed policy models:  
   *"Open one pair from your bundle. Wear it, wash it, sleep in it. If you are not completely satisfied with your overnight protection and peace of mind, keep that first pair and return the remaining unopened pairs for a full refund."*  
   *Commercial Governance Note:* All consumer warranty terms, refund conditions, and satisfaction guarantees represent **proposed marketing concepts only**. No commercial guarantee or trial policy may be deployed or advertised without separate, explicit operator sign-off.
   *Risk Modeling:* By reserving a 3.5% return allowance ($2.45/hero bundle), return claims are fully funded in financial models without degrading unit profitability.
2. **Post-Purchase Upsell Architecture (Increasing Cart Value):**
   - *Pre-Purchase In-Cart Bump:* Add a Delicates Laundry Mesh Wash Bag for +$6.95 (COGS $0.85 = +$6.10 margin contribution). Expected take rate: 22%.
   - *Post-Purchase One-Click Upsell (Post-Checkout):* "Add a 4th Sleep Short to your order for just $18.00 (Save 40%)" (COGS $4.85 + $0.50 shipping = +$12.65 margin contribution). Expected take rate: 14%.
3. **Natural Replacement & LTV Cycle:**  
   Reusable period garments naturally experience elastic and membrane fatigue after 12 to 18 months of monthly laundering. Automated email sequences triggered at Month 9 offer repeat customers a "Cycle Refresh Bundle" at a 20% loyalty discount, capturing $0-CAC repeat orders with high retention value.
