QUESTION 1: WHAT PRECISE PAIN POINT ARE WE SOLVING?
====================================================================================================
We are solving the "Back-Gap Leakage Catastrophe" that terrorizes heavy-flow women when sleeping on 
their backs. In the supine position, gravity channels menstrual fluid down the intergluteal cleft. 
Standard pads and legacy period panties leave a 3-to-4-inch unprotected gap of plain fabric below

the crinkly plastic noise, thigh chafing, and clammy "diaper feeling."

====================================================================================================
QUESTION 2: WHY WILL OUR PROPOSED OEM PRODUCT BE MEANINGFULLY DIFFERENT?
====================================================================================================
Our product refuses to compete in the saturated low-end "lifestyle bikini" segment or the $1.05/pair 
disposable paper pull-up market (Always ZZZ). Instead, we bridge the gap between heavy-duty high-absorbency 
protection and buttery-soft luxury loungewear. Unlike Knix, Saalt, or Bambody (which leave rear gaps), 
and unlike Revol Cares (which produces a stiff, bulky, $55 medicinal garment), our sleep short 
provides an unbroken, waistband-integrated rear barrier in an ultra-slim 2.9 mm profile that feels 
silent, cloud-soft, and completely non-bulky.

====================================================================================================
QUESTION 3: WHAT PHYSICAL ENGINEERING MECHANISM SUPPORTS THAT DIFFERENCE?
====================================================================================================
The "Spine-Shield Architecture":
1. A continuous 44 cm multi-layer composite running from the front pelvic seam, through a widened 
   12 cm crotch, flaring to 15.5 cm across the buttocks, and terminating directly inside the folded 
   rear waistband elastic seam (0.0 mm of exposed unlined fabric).
2. A two-stage fluid acquisition core: an 80 GSM needle-punched ADL that pulls heavy gushes down in 
   under two seconds, feeding a 280 GSM microfiber terry core targeting 60–80 mL total capacity.
3. Stepped-down edge tapering that removes bulky padding right at the spine while maintaining a 
   continuous waterproof 0.015 mm breathable TPU barrier against liquid strikethrough.

====================================================================================================
QUESTION 4: HOW DO WE COMMUNICATE THE DIFFERENCE THROUGH THREE META CREATIVES?
====================================================================================================
We deploy three materially distinct, direct-response 9:16 vertical video assets:
- Creative A ("The Back-Gap Reveal"): Visual tape-measure hook exposing the 3-inch gap on ordinary 
  underwear, followed by the inside-out flip proving our continuous waistband shield and the 
  30-degree incline gravity demonstration.
- Creative B ("The Heavy-Flow Moment"): Scientific pattern-interrupt challenging slow wicking, 
  demonstrating our GushLock Core drinking a sudden 15 mL fluid surge in under 2 seconds, backed by 
  a 5 lb weight dry-blotting test.
- Creative C ("No More Bulky Protection"): Sensory ASMR contrast between a noisy, crinkly plastic 
  disposable pad and the dead silence of buttery bamboo modal, proving 6-pad capacity in a 2.9 mm short.

====================================================================================================
QUESTION 5: WHAT MUST OUR MANUFACTURER ACTUALLY BUILD AND PROVE BEFORE LAUNCH?
====================================================================================================
Before commercial inventory is ordered or performance claims are published, the OEM factory must:
1. Fabricate 3 physical prototypes (V1 Coverage, V2 Speed, V3 Balanced) meeting PRD dimensions.
2. Furnish an accredited lab test report (SGS/Intertek) demonstrating Total Fluorine < 20 ppm 
   (CEN/TS 17043 / ASTM D7359) proving compliance with California AB 1817 and California AB 2515.
3. Pass seam seal impermeability under 2.0 psi static pressure (AATCC 127 / ASTM F1670 modified).
4. Validate 50-cycle home laundering durability (AATCC 135) with zero TPU membrane delamination.
====================================================================================================
