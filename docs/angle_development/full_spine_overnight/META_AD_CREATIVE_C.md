   - One production prototype of *Full-Spine 360° Overnight Sleep Short* (Size M, Plum Berry).
   - Calipers / digital micrometer or small acrylic ruler.
   - Cozy, aesthetic bedroom setup with warm ambient lighting (3200K–4000K).
2. **Real Product Demonstration Protocols (Fairness & Integrity):**
   - The audio comparison must feature honest acoustic recording without artificially boosting the pad audio or artificially muting the sleep shorts.
