Dear Factory Team,

We are developing a premium, high-absorbency reusable period sleep short engineered specifically for the US market. Our design features an unbroken, 4-layer extended gusset that runs continuously from the front pelvic seam to the top rear waistband elastic.

Please review our technical requirements below and complete this questionnaire so our engineering team can evaluate your production capabilities, sample timelines, and volume quotation.

---

### SECTION 1: MATERIAL AVAILABILITY & SOURCING

1. **Top Skin-Contact Wicking Layer:**
   - Can you supply a certified **95% Organic Cotton, 5% Spandex Honeycomb / Birdseye Mesh knit (160–180 GSM)** in jet-black?
   - Is this cotton GOTS certified or OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class II (or Class I) certified?
2. **Fast Fluid Acquisition Layer (ADL):**
   - Can you source a **needle-punched hydrophilic polyester webbing (70–90 GSM)** to serve as an internal fluid distribution layer?
3. **High-Capacity Absorbent Core:**
   - Can you supply a **high-density microfiber terry knit (80% polyester, 20% polyamide, 260–300 GSM)**?
   - What is the maximum liquid absorption capacity (in grams per square meter or mL per garment) your standard absorbent core can achieve without exceeding 3.0 mm total dry thickness?
4. **Waterproof Breathable Membrane (TPU):**
   - Do you source **0.015 mm (15-micron) monolithic ether-grade Thermoplastic Polyurethane (TPU) film**?
   - Can your supplier verify that this membrane achieves a hydrostatic water column resistance $\ge 10,000\text{ mm H}_2\text{O}$ (AATCC 127) and a Moisture Vapor Transmission Rate (MVTR) $\ge 3,000\text{ g/m}^2/\text{24h}$ (ASTM E96 Inverted Cup BW)?
5. **Outer Body Shell:**
   - Can you supply a **90% Bamboo Modal / MicroModal, 10% Spandex jersey knit (190–210 GSM)** in Carbon Black, Midnight Navy, and Plum Berry?

---

### SECTION 2: SEAM SEALING & BONDING CAPABILITIES

6. **Ultrasonic Welding & Seam Taping:**
   - What machinery do you possess for bonding the perimeter of the waterproof gusset?
     - [ ] High-frequency ultrasonic welding (applicable to synthetic core/membrane layers)
     - [ ] Heat-activated polyurethane seam-sealing tape (e.g., Bemis / Framis 0.08 mm PU tape)
     - [ ] Standard cut-and-sew overlock only
   - *Note: Standard lockstitch sewing that punctures the TPU layer without seam taping is unacceptable for our leakproof standards. Natural cotton/modal shell fabric requires seam tape or synthetic barrier bonding.*
7. **Waistband Attachment:**
   - Can your assembly line insert the top edge of the rear absorbent gusset directly into the folded 3.5 cm waistband elastic with zero gap of unlined shell fabric?
   - Can you perform stepped-down tapering (thinning out the absorbent core 1.5 cm before the waistband while continuing the waterproof membrane and topsheet into the seam)?

### SECTION 3: CHEMICAL COMPLIANCE & ACCREDITED CERTIFICATIONS

8. **PFAS & California AB 2515 / AB 1817 Compliance:**
   - Have you or your lamination mill conducted **Total Fluorine (TF)** screening or **Total Organic Fluorine (TOF)** testing on your raw materials and laminated textiles?

   - If Total Fluorine is detected, can you provide targeted LC-MS/MS or TOF speciation demonstrating zero intentionally added PFAS and compliance with California AB 2515 (enacted statutory limits and applicable DTSC determinations) and California AB 1817? *(Note: Total fluorine screening is a preliminary tool and not proof of zero PFAS; laboratory-recommended complementary speciation is mandatory if TF is detected).*
