## 7. MANUFACTURING LIMITATIONS & FACTORY CONSULTATION POINTS

The factory must review and formally acknowledge the following engineering features:
1. **Stepped-Down Waistband Attachment:** Layer 3 (Ultra-Lock Core) must terminate 15 mm below the top edge of the rear waistband. Layers 1, 2, 4, 5, and the outer modal chassis must continue into the folded waistband and be anchored via 4-thread flatlock stitching.
2. **SideSeal™ Barrier Construction:** The dual lateral barriers (1.5 cm width on left and right perineal margins) must utilize a water-repellent (fluorocarbon-free DWR) bonded micro-ribbing to prevent fluid from overflowing laterally when sleeping on the side.
3. **Seam-Tape Sealing Machine Capability:** All perimeter needle punctures surrounding the gusset must be heat-taped using 0.08 mm polyurethane tape (Bemis 3415 or equivalent) to prevent capillary seam weepage.
