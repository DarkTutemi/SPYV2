## 5. MANUFACTURING PROCESS & ASSEMBLY REQUIREMENTS

1. **Precision Stepped-Down Waistband Tapering:**
   - The 280 GSM microfiber terry core (Layer 3) terminates strictly **1.5 cm below the rear waistband casing**.
   - Only Layer 1 (organic cotton mesh) and Layer 4 (0.015 mm TPU film) continue into the folded waistband seam.
   - The waistband is finished using a 4-needle 6-thread flatlock stitch (`ISO 4915 Stitch Type 607`) with 12–14 SPI.
2. **Hybrid Anti-Leak Seam Sealing:**
   - The perimeter of Layer 4 TPU is bonded using heat-activated 0.08 mm polyurethane seam tape (Bemis 3415 or equivalent, $135^\circ\text{C}$, 4 bar pressure, 12s dwell) to prevent needle-hole capillary leaking along the crotch and thigh edges.
3. **Dot Lamination Matrix:**
   - Lamination of TPU to microfiber core and outer shell must utilize solvent-free PUR hot-melt adhesive applied in a breathable dot grid pattern ($12\text{ g/m}^2$). Cured in a humidity chamber for 48 hours prior to garment cutting.
4. **Leg Cuffs:**
   - Fold-over self-fabric binding with encased 0.5 mm mobilon elastic tape. Must prevent leg edge rolling without forming a tourniquet band on the quadriceps.

## 7. PROTOTYPE ACCEPTANCE BENCH TESTS

1. **Incline Run-Off Test:** Mounted on a 30-degree incline, 25 mL saline dosed at 5 mL/s must be captured with **0.0 mL fluid escape** over the rear waistband edge.
2. **Compression Rewet Press Test:** After 40 mL fluid delivery and 2 minutes of 0.5 psi load, dry blotting paper must exhibit $\le 0.50\text{ g}$ moisture transfer.
3. **Waistband Tactile Transition:** Zero perceptible ridge or hardness at the sacrum when lying flat.
4. **Hydrostatic Resistance:** Unseamed TPU and sealed seams must resist $\ge 10,000\text{ mm H}_2\text{O}$ (AATCC 127).

## 8. ENGINEERING RISKS & MITIGATION MATRIX

| Engineering Risk | Severity | Root Cause | Factory Mitigation Protocol |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Achieving $\le 3.0$ mm Dry Thickness** | High | Standard terry loops can fluff up, exceeding 3.2 mm | Mandate high-density, low-pile micro-sheared terry (280 GSM) |
| **Factory Stepped-Down Taper Alignment** | Medium-High | Operator misalignment can cause terry to enter waistband casing | Provide clear notch alignment markings on CNC pattern cutouts |
| **Seam Tape Stiffening on Curves** | Medium | Heat tape can become rigid on curved perineal edges | Specify micro-beveled, ultra-pliable 0.08 mm PU tape (Bemis 3415) |
