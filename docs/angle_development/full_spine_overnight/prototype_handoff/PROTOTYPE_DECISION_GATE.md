## 3. STRICT PROJECT KILL-SWITCH CRITERIA (GO / NO-GO)

The project will be **immediately halted or the supplier disqualified** if any of the following catastrophic failure modes occur during prototype testing:

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| KILL-SWITCH FAILURE CRITERIA                                      | TRIGGER SEVERITY | ACTION      |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Intentionally Added PFAS / Statutory Violation (CA AB 2515)   | FATAL LEGAL RISK | Disqualify  |
| 2. Seam Hydrostatic Failure (Needle holes leaking at < 5,000 mm)  | FATAL DEFECT     | Reject PO   |
| 3. TPU Membrane Delamination (< 10 home wash cycles)              | FATAL QUALITY    | Re-Laminate |
| 4. Dry Gusset Thickness Exceeds 4.0 mm (Severe Diaper Bulk)       | POSITIONING KILL | Re-Engineer |
| 5. Scaled Factory FOB Quote Exceeds $6.20 USD (at 2,500 units)    | UNIT ECON COLLAPSE| Re-Quote   |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Fatal Chemical Contamination (PFAS Gate):**
   - If laboratory testing (ISO 20999:2026 screening followed by accredited LC-MS/MS / TOF speciation) detects intentionally added PFAS, or regulated PFAS at or above applicable DTSC enforcement thresholds under California AB 2515 (or AB 1817 limits), the mill is **immediately disqualified**. No product violating California PFAS statutes will enter the US market.
2. **Fatal Construction Leakage (Seam Failure):**
   - If the perimeter seam exhibits capillary liquid leaking through needle punctures under static pressure ($< 5,000\text{ mm H}_2\text{O}$), and the factory cannot provide certified heat-activated PU seam taping, the prototype program is paused until seam-sealing tooling is resolved.
3. **Severe Delamination (Wash Failure):**
   - If the TPU waterproof membrane blisters, bubbles, or peels away from the absorbent core in fewer than 10 home laundering cycles, commercial ordering is prohibited.
4. **Unit Economic Infeasibility (FOB Price Spike):**
   - If the factory's final volume quotation at 2,500 units exceeds **$6.20 USD per unit**, eroding the Hero 3-pack break-even CPA below $41.00 USD, commercial production is deferred until raw material sourcing is re-negotiated.
