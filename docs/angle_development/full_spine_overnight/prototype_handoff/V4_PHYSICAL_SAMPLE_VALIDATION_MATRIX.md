## 2. DETAILED TEST PROTOCOLS & ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

### PROTOCOL 1: TOTAL ATMOSPHERIC ABSORPTION CAPACITY (ULTRA-LOCK CORE™)
- **Governing Standard:** ISO 9073-6:2025 (Modified for finished composite underwear).
- **Scope Distinction:** Measures total atmospheric fluid absorption capacity under free immersion. Serves as maximum fluid retention benchmark.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Weigh conditioned dry garment ($W_{\text{dry}}$).
  2. Submerge absorbent zone in 0.9% physiological saline at $23^\circ\text{C}$ for 20 minutes.
  3. Hang specimen on 30° drain rig to drip freely for 2.0 minutes.
  4. Weigh wet garment ($W_{\text{wet}}$) and calculate: $\text{Capacity (mL)} = \frac{W_{\text{wet}} - W_{\text{dry}}}{1.005\text{ g/mL}}$.
- **Pass Threshold:** $\ge 150.0\text{ mL}$ total fluid retention (Variant V4 Flagship Target: 150–160 mL).
- **Current Status:** `PENDING PHYSICAL SAMPLE FABRICATION` (Actual value to be recorded upon bench execution).

### PROTOCOL 2: LIQUID RETENTION UNDER BODY-WEIGHT PRESSURE (COMPRESSION ASSAY)
- **Governing Standard:** EDANA WSP 080.4.R3 (Adapted for supine human body pressure).
- **Apparatus:** Calibrated 0.5 psi ($3.45\text{ kPa}$) mechanical load plate (simulating the localized pelvic pressure of a 150–180 lb woman sleeping in supine posture).
- **Procedure:**
  1. Dose center perineum of garment with 75.0 mL blue saline solution.
  2. Wait 2.0 minutes for full distribution into Layer 3 Ultra-Lock Core.
  3. Place pre-weighed stack of 10 sheets Whatman No. 1 filter paper on the topsheet.
  4. Apply 0.5 psi load plate for 120 seconds.
  5. Remove filter paper and weigh immediately.
- **Pass Threshold:** Fluid transferred to blotter $\le 0.30\text{ grams}$.
- **Current Status:** `PENDING PHYSICAL SAMPLE FABRICATION`.

### PROTOCOL 3: SURFACE REWET & DRY-FEEL INTERVALS (DRYTOUCH MATRIX™)
- **Governing Standard:** SPYV2 Standard Surface Dryness Assay.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Deliver 20.0 mL fluid dose (simulating sudden nocturnal menstrual surge).
  2. Measure strike-through time (time until fluid disappears below topsheet).
  3. Conduct blotter pick-up test at $t = 5\text{ minutes}$, $t = 15\text{ minutes}$, and $t = 30\text{ minutes}$.
- **Pass Threshold:**
  * Strike-through acquisition time: $< 1.5\text{ seconds}$.
  * Surface rewet pick-up at 15 minutes: $\le 0.20\text{ grams}$.
- **Current Status:** `PENDING PHYSICAL SAMPLE FABRICATION`.

### PROTOCOL 4: SIDE-SLEEPER LEAKAGE RESISTANCE (SIDESEAL GUARD™)
- **Governing Standard:** SPYV2 Mechanical Lateral Rig (Side-Sleep Simulation).
- **Apparatus:** Anatomical pelvic half-mannequin rotated 45° laterally on its hip axis.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Mount dry prototype garment onto lateral mannequin.
  2. Position delivery nozzle directly against inner thigh fold along the SideSeal™ barrier.
  3. Rapidly dose 15.0 mL blue saline at $3.0\text{ mL / second}$.
  4. Observe whether fluid escapes across the lateral barrier onto the outer modal leg fabric.
- **Pass Threshold:** **0.0 mL fluid escape** (Zero droplet staining beyond the 1.5 cm SideSeal barrier).
- **Current Status:** `PENDING PHYSICAL SAMPLE FABRICATION`.

### PROTOCOL 6: WET BULK, DIMENSIONAL EXPANSION & FIT STABILITY
- **Governing Standard:** ASTM D1777 (Thickness of Textile Materials) + Wear-Sag Protocol.
- **Procedure:**
  1. Measure dry gusset thickness across 5 anatomical points using digital micrometer under $1.0\text{ kPa}$ nominal pressure.
  2. Dose garment with 50 mL, 100 mL, and 150 mL fluid increments; re-measure thickness at each increment.
  3. Suspend loaded garment on mannequin for 6 hours; measure crotch vertical droop/sag.
- **Pass Threshold:**
  * Unworn dry thickness: $\le 3.8\text{ mm}$.
  * Fully saturated wet thickness (150 mL load): $\le 6.8\text{ mm}$.
  * Vertical droop/sag after 6 hours: $< 1.0\text{ cm}$.
- **Current Status:** `PENDING PHYSICAL SAMPLE FABRICATION`.
