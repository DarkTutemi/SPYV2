## 5. MANUFACTURING PROCESS & EMBOSSING REQUIREMENTS

1. **Ultrasonic Channel Quilting:**
   - Layers 2A, 2B, and Layer 3 must be bonded together prior to TPU assembly using **ultrasonic pin embossing** (3 longitudinal lines, 1.5 mm weld dot width).
   - *Critical Constraint:* The ultrasonic quilting horn must NOT contact or puncture the Layer 4 TPU membrane. Lamination to TPU occurs strictly after core quilting via solvent-free PUR adhesive dot pattern.
2. **Waistband Stepped-Down Insertion:**
   - The quilted core sub-assembly (Layers 2A, 2B, 3) terminates **1.5 cm below the waistband**.
   - Only Layer 1 (cotton topsheet) and Layer 4 (TPU membrane) enter the folded waistband seam.
3. **Perimeter Seam Sealing:**
   - Heat-activated PU seam tape (0.08 mm) sealing around the core perimeter before final flatlock body assembly.

## 7. PROTOTYPE ACCEPTANCE BENCH TESTS

1. **High-Speed Gush Challenge:** 15 mL saline dosed from 1 cm height at 10 mL/s must fully penetrate the topsheet in $< 1.5\text{ seconds}$ with zero lateral liquid rolling off the gusset edge.
2. **Channel Diffusion Test:** Under top-down macro imaging, delivered fluid must spread at least 2.5× further along the longitudinal axis than across the transverse axis, proving channel efficacy.
3. **Quilting Integrity Post-Wash:** Ultrasonic quilting lines must exhibit zero thread breaks or fiber separation after 10 preliminary test wash cycles.

## 8. ENGINEERING RISKS & MITIGATION MATRIX

| Engineering Risk | Severity | Root Cause | Factory Mitigation Protocol |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TPU Puncture During Core Quilting** | High | Calibrating ultrasonic horn too deep or laminating TPU before quilting | Strict sequencing: Quilt Layers 2+3 first; laminate TPU in secondary PUR step |
| **Increased Dry Rigidity from Dual ADL** | Medium | Stacking two 60 GSM non-wovens can create paper-like stiffness | Specify low-binder thermal-bonded polyester webs with soft flex hand |
| **Slightly Reduced Total Capacity vs V1** | Medium | 260 GSM core holds less fluid than 320 GSM V1 | Relies on high wicking speed rather than massive storage; positioned for day-two gushes |
