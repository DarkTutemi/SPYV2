## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & SCOPE OF SOURCING

We are engineering a proprietary, high-performance reusable menstrual sleep short engineered specifically for the US consumer market. The product solves the "Back-Gap Problem" (overnight leakage in the supine/back-sleeping posture) through an unbroken, extended gusset that runs continuously from the front pelvic apex into the folded rear waistband casing, supplemented with 360° SideSeal™ inner-thigh lateral protection.

Our **Primary Commercial Production Target is Variant V4 (Full-Spine Ultra Overnight)**, supported by three comparative benchmark iterations (V1, V2, V3).

We require a qualified intimate apparel OEM manufacturing partner capable of:
1. Sourcing certified materials (OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class II, GOTS organic cotton, 0.018 mm breathable TPU, bio-mineral odor matrix).
2. Fabricating a **Four-Piece Prototype Sample Set (1 pc V1, 1 pc V2, 1 pc V3, 1 pc V4 in Size M)** according to our technical briefs.
3. Providing tiered volume quotation and scaled manufacturing for commercial rollout (1,000 to 10,000 units), prioritizing Variant V4.

### 3.4 Chemical Safety & Raw Material Certifications
The US market (particularly California) enforces strict statutory limits on fluorinated substances and toxic chemicals:
- **California AB 2515 (Menstrual Products Right to Know and PFAS Prohibition Act):** Prohibits intentionally added PFAS effective January 1, 2025. Effective January 1, 2027, prohibits products with regulated PFAS at or above practical quantitation limits or enforcement thresholds determined by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
- **California AB 1817 (Textiles):** Prohibits PFAS in apparel (Total Organic Fluorine $< 100\text{ ppm}$ in 2025; $< 50\text{ ppm}$ in 2027).
- **Inquiry & Verification Requirements:**
  1. **Total Fluorine Screening & Complementary Testing:** Can your mill provide an accredited lab report (SGS, Intertek, TÜV) demonstrating Total Fluorine below the laboratory reporting limit via Combustion Ion Chromatography (**ISO 20999:2026, CEN/TS 17043, or ASTM D7359**)? If Total Fluorine is detected, can the facility arrange accredited complementary testing (targeted LC-MS/MS and Extractable/Total Organic Fluorine) to prove absence of intentionally added or regulated PFAS?
  2. Does your raw material supply chain hold valid **OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 Class II** certification for skin-contact intimates?
  3. Is the organic cotton topsheet certified under **GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard)**?
