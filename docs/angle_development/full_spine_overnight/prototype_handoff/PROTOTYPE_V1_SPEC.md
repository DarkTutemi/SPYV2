## 5. MANUFACTURING PROCESS & ASSEMBLY REQUIREMENTS

1. **Precision Die-Cutting:** All 4 internal gusset plies must be CNC die-cut with smooth anatomical curves matching the 17.5 cm rear gluteal flare.
2. **Stepped-Down Waistband Attachment:**
   - The heavy 320 GSM terry retention core (Layer 3) must terminate **1.5 cm below the waistband seam**.
   - Only Layer 1 (cotton topsheet) and Layer 4 (TPU film) continue into the folded waistband casing.
   - Attached via 4-needle 6-thread flatlock stitching (`ISO 4915 Type 607`) with differential feed tension to prevent puckering.
3. **Perimeter Seam Sealing:**
   - Since natural organic cotton cannot be ultrasonically welded without burning, the TPU perimeter must be bonded to the ADL using heat-activated 0.08 mm polyurethane tape (Bemis 3415 or equivalent, $135^\circ\text{C}$, 4 bar, 12s dwell) prior to body shell overlocking.
4. **Leg Cuff Assembly:**
   - 2.5 cm self-fabric fold-over binding encasing 0.6 mm mobilon elastic tape. Must prevent leg edge rolling without forming a tourniquet band on the quadriceps.

- **Rewet Under 0.5 psi Pressure:** $\le 0.65\text{ grams}$.

## 7. PROTOTYPE ACCEPTANCE BENCH TESTS

Before advancing V1 to user wear trials, the prototype sample must pass:
1. **Incline Run-Off Test:** Mounted on a 30-degree incline, 30 mL synthetic saline dosed at 5 mL/s must be captured with **0.0 mL fluid escape** over the rear waistband edge.
2. **Waistband Bulge Tactile Audit:** When laid flat against an acrylic mannequin back, the stepped-down seam transition must measure $< 1.5\text{ mm}$ step height, avoiding lumbar ridge pressure.
3. **Seam Hydrostatic Challenge:** Seamed perimeter must resist $\ge 10,000\text{ mm H}_2\text{O}$ without needle-puncture weeping (AATCC 127).

## 8. ENGINEERING RISKS & MITIGATION MATRIX

| Engineering Risk | Severity | Root Cause | Factory Mitigation Protocol |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lumbar Bulge / Ridge Sensation** | High | 320 GSM terry + 17.5 cm wide flare stacking against spine | Strict enforcement of 1.5 cm stepped-down taper before waistband |
| **Garment Weight "Diaper Sensation"** | Medium-High | 138g total weight exceeds consumer comfort benchmark (120g) | Evaluate micro-sheared terry loop to shave 10% weight without losing capacity |
| **Thermal Heat Retention** | Medium | Large 17.5 cm surface area reduces breathability across buttocks | Mandate ether-grade TPU with certified MVTR $\ge 2,800\text{ g/m}^2/\text{24h}$ |
