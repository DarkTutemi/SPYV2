How Activated Carbon Filter is supplied

Activated Carbon Filter is a ZHONGHE catalog model produced at its own factory: a replacement cartridge, supplied with ZHONGHE filter shower heads or on its own. Its filter media come from the filter-media supplier, whose third-party reports cover the media, not a finished shower head. Confirm availability, tooling ownership and charges for the selected variant in the quotation. Logo, packaging, color, filter and finish changes are reviewed for the project.
