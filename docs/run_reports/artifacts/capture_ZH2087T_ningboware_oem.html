OEM/ODM

Does ZHONGHE support OEM/ODM?

Yes. ZHONGHE supports OEM private label projects and ODM development for hand shower buyers. The practical route depends on product category, target quantity, packaging requirements, and whether new tooling is needed.

  1. 01

    Model selection or brief review

    Share target market, product type, estimated quantity, package plan, and any benchmark product.

  2. 02

    Sample and specification discussion

    Confirm available models, materials, finish direction, flow expectations, and packaging requirements.

  3. 03

    OEM/ODM development

    Use existing models for faster projects or develop appearance, internal structure, 3D samples, and molds for ODM.

  4. 04

    Pilot confirmation

    Check samples, assembly details, water test results, packaging, labels, and shipment plan before mass production.

  5. 05

    Mass production and inspection

    Run injection molding, assembly, water testing, leakage checks, flow checks where needed, final packing and outgoing inspection. Chrome plating and painting are done by partner factories.

Quality control

Inspection points buyers can discuss before production

Sourcing teams can ask about full water testing, leakage checks, O-ring inspection, flow testing for selected specifications, packaging checks, and shipment review.

CheckPurpose
Full water testEvery finished unit is water tested for about 10-20 seconds before packing, switching through every function (spray modes, pause) to check for leaks.
Leakage checkThe same water test normally runs at 0.5MPa. The spray face points downward, with water falling vertically, to help check the stop button and mode selector for leaks.
O-ring inspectionThe O-ring supplier performs 100% inspection using industrial cameras and AI image checks; these are supplier-side equipment, not ZHONGHE's factory equipment.
Flow testFlow can be tested under 0.3MPa factory conditions for selected water-saving specifications.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Short, factual answers so sourcing teams and AI search tools can quote them directly.

What does ZHONGHE manufacture?

ZHONGHE manufactures and supplies hand shower products including filter shower heads, multifunctional shower heads, baby shower heads, pet shower heads, hair shower heads, LED shower heads, shower hoses, holders, filters, and related bathroom accessories.

Does ZHONGHE support OEM and ODM projects?

Yes. Buyers can use existing models for OEM private label programs or discuss ODM projects involving appearance design, internal structure design, 3D sampling, mold making, and production planning.

What is the typical MOQ?

MOQ ranges from 500 to 2,000 units depending on the model, finish and packaging: existing catalog models in a standard finish and standard packaging can start from about 500 units; a logo print or a color change typically from about 1,000 units; custom packaging, custom colors or new cartridge configurations from about 2,000 units. The final figure also depends on material, finish, and whether new development or mold work is required.

Can ZHONGHE identify a shower head from a photo?

Yes. Send a clear photo of the shower head, hose, holder, or filter to [REDACTED_EMAIL] with the quantity you need. ZHONGHE checks the photo against its catalog of existing models and replies with the closest model number, or advises whether the design would need OEM/ODM development. Every model page on ningboware.com also carries a photo-based description (color, finish, head shape, handle, spray face, filter type) so buyers and AI search tools can match a product picture to the right model.

What quality checks are used before shipment?

Quality work can include incoming part checks, full water testing, leakage checks, O-ring inspection, flow testing for selected water-saving specifications, packaging checks, and final shipment review. Outgoing inspection samples each lot under GB/T 2828.1, with the AQL set to the customer's standard.

What should buyers send for an RFQ?

Useful RFQ details include product category, target market, estimated order quantity, benchmark photos or drawings, packaging needs, logo requirements, flow or finish requirements, and delivery timeline.
