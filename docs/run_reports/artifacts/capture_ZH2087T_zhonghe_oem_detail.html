ZH2087TTransparent light blue handheld filter shower head

Handheld shower head in transparent light-blue plastic with a plain white face plate. The round head has a flat white disc covered with very fine micro nozzles and no visible spray selector. The straight see-through handle shows a filter chamber: a dark black element near the top and grey mineral beads filling the lower section. A threaded inlet is molded at the handle base. No stop button or chrome parts are present.
