Q&A

Interested in your 320 pocket hole jig for a cabinet build project. The unit shows material thicknesses of 1/2, 3/4 and 1 1/2&quot;. Would I be able to use it for 5/8&quot; thick material? Thanks
Yes, you will use the material thickness gauge to find out what settings you use.
I have the 320. Instructions indicate the material stops can be removed, but is unclear how this is done. Can you explain?
You will slide the bottom up and it should snap off.
I use the pocket hole jig 320 all the time. I am joining mitered 1x4’s . I like this jig a lot, but find that adjustable stops don’t stay put. I’ve resorted to taping them with electric tape, but that is not a permanent fix. Any suggestions?

does this include wood clamps
No, this will not include any clamps.
I have the Kreg Pocket Hole Jig 320, and I&#x27;m building the Built to Last Potting Bench using the instructions from your website. When joining 2x4s and 2x6s, I understand I use both the jig and the drill bit on 1 1/2&quot; setting, however, I&#x27;m confused when the instructions say to change the settings to 1&quot; (to attach the 2x4s to the 5/4x6 boards). There is no 1&quot; setting on the jig. I can position the collar on the drill bit to 1&quot;, but what setting do I use on the jig? My only choices are 1/2&quot;, 3/4&quot;, or 1 1/2&quot;.
When working with lumber of two different thicknesses, you will want to set the jig to the smaller thickness. So usually you will set it to 1&quot; and use the 1-1/2&quot; screw. But since you are using the 320, you will need to use the material thickness gauge and hang it over the edge of the wood per the instructions. That gauge has setting ranges on there. Where ever that lands, you will see the settings needed. For 1&quot;, it will land in the book. Then you will go to the manual to find the settings and screw length. It will let you know you will use the 3/4&quot; settings and the 1-1/2&quot; screw.
I want to construct sturdy doors made of 1x2 lumber to be used in my raised garden to keep squirrels out. The doors will have chicken wire attached to them. I have your 320 tool however the drill bit looks too large for use on a 1x2. Do you have a tool that will work in this situation?
You sure can use the 320 with the 1x2. You will drill the holes with the 3/4&quot; settings and use the 1-1/4&quot; screws.
How do you change the size of the hole? I want to insert a 5/8” dowel for a coat rack.
The 320 only uses a 3/8&quot; bit.
I have a jig 320. One of the gray material stops broke. Where can I get another one?
To get any replacement parts, please give out Tech Team a call at 800-447-8638 M-F 8-5 CST or email them at [REDACTED_EMAIL]
Why does the drill bit not fit with the sample screws included (or any keel screws for that matter)?
That should fit all the screws. If you have not already, please email [REDACTED_EMAIL] and they will help you out.
Difference between k5 and the 720 pro
They will drill the same style holes. The 720 has a self adjusting drill guide for the thickness so no adjustment needed. The bit also has quick set markings for 1/2&quot;, 3/4&quot; and 1-1/2&quot;. The K5, you will need to adjust the drill guide manually and use the setup block and drill guide to set to correct setting.

The complete pocket-hole kit for building DIY wood projects
The Kreg® Pocket-Hole Jig 320 makes it easier than ever to build wood projects — whether you’re building your first DIY project, or you’ve been working with wood for years. The Pocket-Hole Jig 320 works with materials of any thickness between 1/2" and 1 1/2". It features two drill guides, a removable spacer, and stops for 1/2"- , 3/4"-, and 1 1/2"-thick material that give the versatility you need to build with boards and plywood.

The included material thickness gauge and easy-set drill bit make setup simple, while the convenient clamp adapter and anti-slip base hold the jig precisely in position while you drill perfectly positioned pocket holes every time. The jig and its included components store in a durable, compact case that even has space to store Kreg Pocket-Hole Screws — with 40 sample screws included. Durable construction and hardened-steel guides with a lifetime warranty ensure that the Kreg Pocket-Hole Jig 320 will deliver the years of project-building and home-repair satisfaction and value you expect from the #1 pocket-hole brand — Kreg.
